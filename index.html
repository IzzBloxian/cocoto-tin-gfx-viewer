Notes

.PC header: u32 ZSIZE, u32 SIZE. Payload at byte 8.
LZSS: LSB-first flags, len=(hi&0xF)+3, pos=lo|((hi&0xF0)<<4), ring fill 0x20.
TIN: counts at +8/+0xC/+0x10, arrays at +0x20.
Array C: +0 type, +4 sz, +8 width, +0xC offset.
Types: 1 = PSMT8+mip, 5 = PSMT32.
Selection: click = single, ctrl+click = toggle, shift+click = range, ctrl+A = all, esc = clear.
Load a TIN.PC + GFX.PC pair to preview textures.
