Notes

.PC header: u32 ZSIZE, u32 SIZE. Payload at byte 8.
LZSS / LZMA: Handles both PS2 and PC builds.
TIN: counts at +8/+0xC/+0x10, arrays at +0x20.
Array C: +0 type, +4 sz, +8 width, +0xC offset.
Types: 1 = PSMT8+mip, 5 = PSMT32. Bit 31 = pre-swizzled.
Decode: GS unswizzle + palette CSM1/CSM2 swap + sub_474360 bit swap.
